Modified for Custom Anime Thralls: baked pose and transforms, normalized height and origin, sampled materials into vertex colors, welded vertices, removed degenerate triangles, and reduced geometry where required. No endorsement implied.
This model remains under its source CC BY license, separate from the plugin's BSD code.

Animation trial: retained source skin weights, mapped humanoid bones, exported procedural idle/walk/attack clips and approximate wrist-local hand anchors. Rebuilt matching vertex-colour geometry without pose-dependent welding.
Adapted Esdeath pose timing and body choreography from girl_poses.py/export_girl.py to the mapped source rig. Finger curls and secondary hair/cloth motion are omitted.
Removed the duplicate inverted green outline shell to expose the textured dress.
